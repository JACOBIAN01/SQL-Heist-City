# Content rating (Phase 21.7)

**What the owner has to do**: the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) questionnaire can only be submitted by the publisher (inputs-needed #12). It produces ratings for PEGI, ESRB, USK, ClassInd, ACB and others at once. Nothing has been submitted. Below are the answers the game's actual content supports, so the form takes minutes and is truthful. Check them against the game before submitting.

## The game as it is
- Third-person 3D, in a cartoon-style city, played in a browser by classes of up to 200 students. Each student has a character with a display name.
- To open a vault lock, heal, get a gun or ammo, a student solves a SQL question in a pop-up while the world keeps running.
- Other students' characters can **shoot** them (pistols, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, snipers) and **run them over** with cars. A character that runs out of health "dies" and respawns after a short delay, losing the cash it carried. There is no way to play without this.
- No blood, gore, dismemberment or lingering corpses (verify in the build before submitting; the rating depends on it). No sexual content, nudity, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, or crude language in the game text; student display names are filtered with a word list.
- Players can see each other's chosen display names. **There is no text or voice chat** and no way to send messages between players.
- No in-game purchases, no real-money gambling, no advertising, no loot boxes. No location sharing. Sign-in with Google; data as in the privacy policy.

## Expected outcome (to be confirmed by the questionnaire, not claimed here)
Cartoon shooting at stylised characters without blood typically rates around **PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen / USK 12**. Schools with younger students should see the rating and decide (the consent text says so). The administrator's minimum age setting (default 13) should not be below the rating's age.

## After the rating
1. Put the rating and descriptors in the privacy policy ("The game is violent" paragraph) and the Terms.
2. Set `minAge` in Settings → Privacy to at least the lowest age the ratings allow.
3. Keep the certificate: schools may ask for it.
